How the tiers work. S means a must-pick that deserves a ban. A is the meta core. B is situational. C needs a comp built around it. Diamond+ and low elo are scored separately because the same hero does not perform the same way in both lobbies.

Reading the numbers. Win rate, pick rate and ban rate all come from the same sample: competitive, Diamond and above, final week of Season 10.5. Ban rates only exist from Gold III upward, so there is no low-elo ban column. A MUST BAN or MUST PICK ribbon is an editorial call for 11.0, not a stat.
